About Me

I enjoy learning how websites are created and how design can make information easier and more enjoyable to understand. I also like drawing, cooking, and being creative in my free time.

I am especially interested in learning HTML, CSS, and other web design skills. My goal is to become more confident in design and create websites that are simple, useful, and visually appealing.

I am currently studying at AC and working toward my goals one step at a time. My first year of college taught me that learning takes patience, practice, and consistency.

My goal is to continue learning and become better at what I do. I hope that in the future I can use my design skills to create meaningful digital experiences. I believe that small steps can lead to big changes, and I want to keep moving forward even when something feels difficult. HTML5 is one of the skills I am learning as part of my web design studies.
